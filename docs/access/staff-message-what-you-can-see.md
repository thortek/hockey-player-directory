# What you can see in the hockey operations app

Welcome. This note explains what the app shows you, based on your job. You sign in with your own account, and the app shows you what your job needs.

## If you are the assistant scout

You can see every scouting observation our scouts have written, the game schedule and results, and the roster with names, numbers, and positions. You can add your own observations from the games you attend.

You cannot change notes someone else wrote. Health, injury, medical, and sports-science information stays closed to your job. That includes the scouting player board and every other shared screen: you will not see injury or health notes there, and you won't see labels like "injured" or "out" next to a player's name.

## If you are the video analyst

You can see the scouting observations, the game schedule and results, and the roster, so you can find the moments scouts flagged and label your clips with the right player and game.

You can look, but you cannot add or change anything. Health, injury, medical, and sports-science information is not part of your job and does not appear for you anywhere, including on the shared scouting player board and other shared scouting or game screens.

## If you already work here

- **Coaches:** nothing changes. You still see the roster, the schedule, and scouting observations.
- **Scouts:** nothing changes. You still see and record scouting observations. Health and sports-science information stays with medical staff, as before.
- **Medical and sports-science staff:** nothing changes. You still see and record health, injury, and training information. It is still yours alone to see.

## If something looks wrong

If you think you should see something you can't, or you see something that looks like it belongs to another job, tell hockey operations. Please don't share your account or ask a colleague to look something up for you.

We will never ask you for your password.
